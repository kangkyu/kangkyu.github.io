LiningLink Reports

Monthly YouTube performance reports, built as Google Slides presentations in your own Google Drive.

A product of Lining Link LLC, Pasadena, California. The application runs at app.lininglink.com.

What LiningLink Reports does

LiningLink Reports is for businesses that report on their YouTube channels every month. Each month, with one click, it builds a Google Slides presentation with your channel's performance and saves it in your Google Drive.

How it works

  1. Sign in with Google. Your Google account identifies you to the app, and you allow it to open the Google Drive files you pick. Access is limited to people your organization has invited.
  2. Pick a template. You choose one presentation in a Google file picker. The app can open only that file and the reports it creates from it.
  3. Connect YouTube, once for your organization. You choose the Google account that owns your channel. The app reads the channel's analytics; it never changes anything on the channel.
  4. Generate a report. Pick a month, click once, and the finished Google Slides presentation appears in your Drive a minute later.

The Google permissions it asks for, and why

When you connect, Google shows you exactly what the app may access. Each permission is used for one purpose:

  • Your email address (sign in with Google): to recognize you when you sign in and to show who generated each report. Nothing is sent to this address without your request.
  • Google Drive files you open with this app (sign in with Google): to read the template you pick, to copy it, and to write each report into your Drive. The app cannot see, search or open any other file in your Drive.
  • YouTube Analytics reports, including monetary reports (read-only): to read views, watch time, subscribers, engagement, traffic sources, search terms, audience demographics and estimated revenue for the month being reported.
  • Your YouTube account (read-only): to list the channel you own and to look up the titles, publish dates and lengths of the videos named in the report.

Report data is used only while your report is being built, then discarded. The only copy is the presentation in your own Google Drive. Your Google authorizations are stored encrypted, and you can revoke them in the app at any time on the Google access page, which removes the stored tokens and ends the app's access at Google. LiningLink Reports' use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
