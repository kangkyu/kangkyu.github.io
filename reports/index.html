LiningLink Reports

Monthly YouTube performance reports, built as Google Slides presentations in your own Google Drive.

What it does

LiningLink Reports is a service for businesses that report on their YouTube channels every month. You give it your own report template (a Google Slides presentation, with your layout, branding and page size) and pick a folder in your Drive. Each month, with one click, it fills a copy of that template with your channel's numbers, tables and charts and saves it in your folder.

  • Views, watch time, subscribers and engagement, with the change from the previous month
  • Top videos, revenue and top revenue drivers
  • Traffic sources, top search terms, and audience age and gender

How it uses your Google data

  • Google Slides: the app can open only the template and folder you pick, and the reports it creates. It can't see any other file in your Drive.
  • YouTube: read-only access to the analytics, estimated revenue and public video details of the channel you choose, used only to fill your reports. Nothing on your channel is ever changed.
  • Nothing is kept: report data is used only while your report is built. The only copy is the presentation in your own Drive. Your connection is stored encrypted, and you can revoke it at any time.

Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Getting access

LiningLink Reports is available to business customers of Lining Link LLC. To set up reports for your channel, contact admin@lininglink.com.
