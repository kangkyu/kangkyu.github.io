Privacy Policy — LiningLink Reports

Effective November 17, 2026

LiningLink Reports ("the Service") is operated by Lining Link LLC ("we", "us"). The Service builds performance reports for business customers: it reads a customer's YouTube Analytics (and, where the customer chooses, data from their own Google BigQuery dataset) and writes each report as a Google Slides presentation into the customer's own Google Drive.

This policy explains what information the Service accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have. The Service is offered to businesses under an agreement with us; it is not intended for personal or consumer use.

1. Information we access and keep

Google authorizations. The Service uses two Google authorizations, each with its own consent screen:

  • Sign-in and Google Slides, granted by each user when they sign in with Google (scopes openid, email, drive.file). We receive your Google account email address and use it only to identify you and the customer you belong to. drive.file lets the Service open only the file you pick with the Google Picker (your template presentation) and the files the Service itself creates for you (each report and its chart data spreadsheet, saved in your My Drive). It gives no access to any other file in your Drive. Reports you request are built on your template, in your Drive.
  • YouTube, granted once per customer with the Connect YouTube button (scopes yt-analytics.readonly, yt-analytics-monetary.readonly, youtube.readonly): lets the Service read, for the channel you choose, YouTube Analytics metrics (such as views, watch time, subscribers, engagement, traffic sources, search terms, audience age and gender), estimated revenue, and public details of your videos and channel (titles, publish dates, durations, channel name). Access is read-only; the Service never changes anything on your channel.

For each authorization we store: the OAuth refresh token, the current access token and when it expires (access tokens last about an hour and are reused until then), and the Google account email that granted it. For YouTube we also store the scopes granted and the chosen channel ID; for each user, the ID of the template file they picked. Tokens are encrypted at rest, with keys held separately in Google Cloud Secret Manager.

BigQuery (optional). If a customer's reports use their own BigQuery data, the customer grants a service account dedicated to that customer read access to the dataset they choose. Queries run only against that dataset, only for that customer's reports.

Report data. Numbers, video details and chart data fetched for a report are held in memory only while that report is being built and are not stored by us. The only lasting copy is the report presentation (and its chart data spreadsheet) in the customer's own Google Drive, which the customer owns and controls.

Run history. For each report run we keep: the report, the period, who requested it, its status or error code, a link to the resulting presentation, when the data was last available, and technical usage counts (such as the number of API requests and the run's duration). Run history contains no report values.

Technical logs. Our hosting provider records standard request logs (such as time, requested page, IP address and browser type) for security and troubleshooting. Logs never contain tokens or report data.

2. How we use information

We use the information above only to provide the Service to the customer who asked for it: to sign users in, to build the reports they request, to show their run history, to notify the people they choose when a report is ready, and to keep the Service secure and working.

We do not sell information, use it for advertising, use it to build profiles, or use it to develop, improve or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models. People at Lining Link LLC do not read your report data, except with your permission to resolve a support request, or where required for security or by law.

Google API data and Limited Use. LiningLink Reports' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Separation between customers

Each customer's connections, settings, reports and run history are kept separate from every other customer's. One customer's tokens are only ever used for that customer's own reports, and each customer's BigQuery access uses a service account that only that customer's data is shared with.

4. Sharing

We do not share your information with anyone except:

  • Service providers that run the Service on our behalf, under confidentiality and security obligations: Google Cloud (hosting, database, secret storage, BigQuery jobs), and, once report notifications are enabled, an email delivery provider.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users or the Service.
  • With your direction, for example the people a customer chooses to notify, who receive a link to the report.

5. YouTube API Services

The Service uses YouTube API Services. By connecting YouTube you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

You can revoke the Service's access to your YouTube and Google data at any time:

  • in the Service, on the Google access page, which lists each Google account behind your organization's authorizations and what it grants; revoking an account there ends everything that account granted the Service, at Google and in the Service; or
  • in your Google Account, at Third-party apps & services.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Tokens and authorization details are deleted when the authorization is revoked or replaced, when an administrator removes the user, or when the customer is deleted; in each case the Service also revokes the access at Google. If access is revoked in your Google Account, the stored token stops working and is deleted when the Service detects it, within 30 days.
  • Customer settings and run history are kept while the customer uses the Service, and deleted within 30 days after the customer's agreement ends or on the customer's written request.
  • Report data is not stored by us (see section 1). Reports in your Drive remain yours and are not deleted by us.
  • Logs are kept for up to 30 days.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Tokens are additionally encrypted at the application level with keys kept in Google Cloud Secret Manager. The database is reachable only over a private network. Access to production systems is limited to Lining Link LLC staff who need it, and changes to the Service are reviewed before deployment.

8. Your choices and rights

Customers and their users can revoke Google authorizations at any time on the Google access page, and may contact us to access, correct or delete the information we hold about them. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights under applicable law; contact us to exercise them.

9. Children

The Service is for businesses and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

10. Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page with a new effective date, and notify customers of material changes in advance.
